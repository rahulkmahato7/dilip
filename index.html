About Me

I am pursuing my M.Tech in Transportation Engineering at the Indian Institute of Technology Roorkee. My academic work focuses on how pedestrians and drivers understand and respond to crossing facilities under India's mixed traffic conditions. I am particularly interested in practical safety improvements informed by road-user perceptions and traffic behavior.

Current Research

Ongoing M.Tech dissertation and related research activities

M.Tech Dissertation | Ongoing

Road Users' Perceptions of Smart Crosswalks in Mixed Traffic

Studying how pedestrians and drivers perceive crossing safety, interpret smart-crosswalk information, and make crossing or yielding decisions in Indian urban traffic.

Research Approach | In Development

Surveys and Traffic Scenarios

Developing pedestrian and driver questionnaires and scenario-based evaluations to compare conventional zebra crossings with proposed smart-crosswalk information and warning systems.

Research Interests

Pedestrian and Driver Behavior

Yielding decisions, gap acceptance, perceived safety, risk perception, and trust in crossing information.

Traffic Safety and Emerging Technologies

Smart crosswalks, vulnerable road-user interactions, traffic conflicts, and technology-supported warning systems.
